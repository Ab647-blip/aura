Admin and dates to keep track of

deadline is August 2027 if there is any travel planned that autumn.

Domain renewals: the main company domain renews on June 12 every year, the two
redirect domains renew in September. All three are on the same registrar account
with autorenew on, but the card on file expires in May so that needs updating first.

Insurance review is due in July. Last year I let it roll over without reading it and
the premium went up eleven percent.

Accountant needs the Q2 numbers by July 15. She has asked for them earlier every year
and every year they arrive on the 14th.
