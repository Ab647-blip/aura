How I like to work

Deep work in the morning, meetings after two in the afternoon. If someone asks for
a morning slot the answer is no unless it is a customer.

I want direct answers. If something is uncertain, say it is uncertain instead of
padding the answer with qualifiers. I would rather be told a plan is risky than be
handed five options with no recommendation.

I do not want reminders for things I have already decided. Remind me about open
questions and deadlines, not about tasks I am clearly on top of.

Email twice a day, not continuously. Slack notifications are off outside of
nine to six on weekdays.

For writing: short sentences, plain words, no filler openings.
